## 📌 Project Overview

The **Sales Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI. It provides insights into sales revenue, product categories, quantity distribution, and geographical sales performance.

The dashboard helps users understand product performance and explore sales distribution across different cities through interactive visualizations.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze total sales revenue across product categories.
- Understand product-wise sales performance.
- Compare quantity distribution across categories.
- Visualize geographical sales performance by city.
- Present business data through interactive dashboards.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Revenue Analysis
- Revenue by Product Category.
- Product-level revenue analysis.
- Category and product hierarchy.

### 2. Quantity Analysis
- Quantity distribution across product categories.
- Category-wise quantity comparison.

### 3. Geographical Sales Analysis
- City-wise sales revenue.
- Map-based sales visualization.
- Geographical distribution of business revenue.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- Data Visualization
- Interactive Charts
- Geographical Mapping
- Business Intelligence

## 📊 Visualizations Used

- Column Chart – Revenue by Category and Product.
- Pie Chart – Quantity by Category.
- Map Visualization – Revenue by City.

- Analyzing sales and revenue data.
- Creating category-wise comparisons.
